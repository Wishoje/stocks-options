# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how GEX Options collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit gexoptions.com or use its dashboards, scanners, calculator, watchlists, exports, account tools, support, and billing flows (collectively, the **Service**). In this policy, **GEX Options**, **we**, **us**, and **our** refer to the operator of the Service under the GEX Options name.

This policy applies to information handled by GEX Options. Stripe, Google, and other independent services also process information under their own privacy notices.

## 1. Information we collect

### Account and security information

When you create or manage an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, password hash, email-verification status, and account timestamps. If you use available security and profile features, we may also process a profile photo, two-factor authentication secret and recovery codes, remember-me status, session identifiers, IP addresses, browser or device information, and recent session activity.

We do not store your password in readable form. You are responsible for keeping passwords and recovery codes confidential.

### Subscription and billing information

Stripe hosts checkout and the billing portal and processes full payment credentials. GEX Options receives and stores limited customer and subscription information needed to provide access and support billing. This can include a Stripe customer or subscription identifier, selected plan and billing interval, subscription and payment status, trial and end dates, and limited payment-method information such as type and last four digits.

We also receive Stripe webhook events and limited transaction facts needed to confirm trial and paid activation, prevent duplicate processing, and maintain subscription state. Provider event identifiers used for internal conversion records are protected with a keyed hash rather than stored in reports in their original form.

### Product and account activity

We process the information you choose in the product, including watchlist symbols and timeframes, requested market symbols, dashboard state, calculator or scanner inputs, export selections, generated export files, and the status and timing of requested work. We may record a limited account-linked event when an authenticated user first inspects a useful dashboard reading. That record is designed to omit the symbol and market value.

The Service may store interface preferences in your browser, including a recently selected calculator symbol and whether an onboarding guide was seen or skipped.

### Contact and support information

If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you submit. If you email support directly, we also collect the email contents, attachments you choose to send, and related correspondence. Do not send passwords, full card numbers, API keys, or other secrets through a support message.

### Device, log, and usage information

When you access the Service, our systems and infrastructure providers may process IP address, browser and device type, operating system, requested URL, referring page, timestamps, session activity, error and security logs, and similar technical information.

When Google Analytics is configured, it collects website-measurement information as described in Section 4. GEX Options also stores limited server-side conversion events, such as first confirmed registration, trial activation, first useful dashboard reading, and paid activation, together with a small set of plan, billing, currency, state, method, or surface labels. To prevent duplicate lifecycle messages, we also record the email event, send time, and limited event context.

### Market data

The Service receives securities and options information from market-data providers and creates derived analytics. Market data about securities is generally not personal information. A symbol can become account-linked information when you add it to a watchlist, request a calculation, or include it in an export.

## 2. How we collect information

We collect information:

- directly from you when you register, update settings, contact support, create a watchlist, enter a calculation, or request an export;

- from Stripe when checkout, billing, trial, payment, cancellation, or subscription events occur; and
- from market-data and infrastructure providers when they return requested data or operational status.

We use personal information to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and support accounts;

- respond to support requests and service communications;

- operate, debug, maintain, monitor, and improve performance and usability;
- measure public-page journeys and confirmed registration, trial, product-use, and paid-activation milestones;
- detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, security incidents, and violations of the Terms of Service;

- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where a law requires a legal basis, we rely as appropriate on performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, compliance with legal obligations, and consent for processing that requires consent. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis, without affecting processing already completed.

## 4. Cookies, browser storage, and Google Analytics

The Service uses cookies and similar storage for functions such as account sessions, CSRF protection, sign-in persistence when you choose **Remember me**, checkout continuity, and security. Blocking essential cookies can prevent registration, sign-in, billing, or authenticated product features from working.

The Service uses local or session storage for limited interface state, such as a recently selected calculator symbol, onboarding status, and markers that prevent the same analytics event from being sent repeatedly. You can clear these values through your browser settings, although product preferences may reset.

When a Google Analytics measurement ID is configured, the Service loads Google's `gtag.js` and sends sanitized page-view and product-journey events. The implementation keeps only approved campaign, plan, billing, route, and interface-state values in custom events. It is designed to exclude names, email addresses, account identifiers, symbols or tickers, strikes, expiration dates, watchlists, support messages, tokens, session identifiers, card or payment values, passwords, and secrets from custom Google Analytics parameters.

Google Analytics can still receive information automatically, including the page URL and referrer supplied after GEX Options filtering, cookie or device identifiers, browser and device details, activity, IP address during collection, and approximate location derived from the IP address. Google states that GA4 uses IP addresses at collection time and discards them before logging them. Google Analytics commonly uses a first-party `_ga` cookie to distinguish visitors. Google's processing and any links to other Google services depend on Google property settings and your Google settings.

You can block or delete cookies through browser controls. Google also provides a [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) and explains [how information is used from sites that use Google services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Blocking analytics does not remove essential account cookies.

## 5. How we disclose information

We disclose information only as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including to:

- **Stripe**, which hosts checkout and billing management, processes payments, prevents fraud, and returns subscription and limited payment information. Stripe may act as a service provider for GEX Options and as an independent controller for some processing. See the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) and [Stripe Privacy Center](https://stripe.com/legal/privacy-center).
- **Google Analytics**, when configured, for audience, journey, and product-use measurement. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its information for [Google Analytics users](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245).
- **Infrastructure, database, storage, security, email, font-delivery, and support providers** that help operate the Service under their service terms and confidentiality or data-protection obligations.
- **Market-data providers** to request and receive symbol, quote, options, and related market information. These server-side requests generally concern financial instruments rather than account identity.
- **Professional advisers and authorities** when reasonably necessary to comply with law, valid legal process, audits, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
- **A successor or transaction participant** in connection with a financing, reorganization, merger, acquisition, asset transfer, or similar business event, subject to appropriate protections and notice when required.

We do not use account profiles, private watchlists, calculator inputs, generated exports, or support messages as a data product for data brokers. Some laws use broad definitions of **sale**, **sharing**, or **targeted advertising**. Depending on Google Analytics settings and applicable law, analytics disclosures may fall within one of those definitions. You may ask us to stop or limit processing that is subject to an applicable opt-out right as described in Section 10.

We may disclose aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you. We will not attempt to re-identify information maintained in de-identified form except to verify that de-identification works or as permitted by law.

## 6. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. The period depends on the type of information, whether your account or subscription remains active, the sensitivity of the data, operational and security needs, provider and backup cycles, dispute and fraud-prevention needs, and legal, tax, accounting, or reporting obligations.

For example, account and product data is generally kept while the account is active; support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve and document requests; security and technical logs are kept according to operational needs; and billing records may remain after account deletion when needed for transaction history, disputes, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Backups may retain deleted information until the applicable backup is overwritten or expires. Stripe applies its own retention practices to information it controls.

When information is no longer needed, we delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it where reasonably practicable.

## 7. Account deletion

You can request account deletion through available account settings. If the account has a saved subscription or billing record, deletion remains unavailable until that record shows a recognized terminal provider status and any saved subscription end date has passed. This prevents a live or unresolved Stripe billing relationship from remaining attached to an inaccessible account.

Deleting an account removes or makes unavailable the account profile and account-linked product data handled through the deletion workflow, including data that is configured to be deleted with the account. Some information can remain for the retention reasons in Section 6, including billing records held by GEX Options or Stripe, support correspondence, security logs, and time-limited backups. Clearing your account does not automatically clear cookies or local storage in your browser; you can do that through browser settings.

## 8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. These include password hashing, encrypted transport, authentication and subscription checks, CSRF protection, rate limiting on sensitive routes, restricted account-level access, and use of Stripe-hosted payment pages so full card credentials are not stored by GEX Options.

No system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, lost, altered, or disclosed without authorization. Use a unique password, protect recovery codes, sign out of devices you do not control, and notify [support@gexoptions.com](mailto:support@gexoptions.com) if you suspect an account or security issue.

## 9. Your choices

Account settings allow you to update available profile information, change your password, review or end supported browser sessions, manage available two-factor authentication, review subscription status, open the Stripe billing portal, and request account deletion when eligible.

Registration and subscription activity can trigger lifecycle email. Some messages are needed for account, trial, payment, cancellation, or security administration; checkout reminders and reactivation messages may be promotional. You may ask [support@gexoptions.com](mailto:support@gexoptions.com) to stop optional promotional messages, and we will honor applicable opt-out rights. We may still send service, security, billing, or legal communications needed to operate your account. Browser controls can block or clear cookies and storage, subject to the functional effects described above.
